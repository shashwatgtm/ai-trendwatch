Source 1: Anthropic Economic Index, India Brief

What It Measures

  • • Actual task-level usage patterns from Claude.ai conversations
  • • Tasks mapped to O*NET occupation codes
  • • Occupation concentration, autonomy delegation, productivity multipliers
  • • Geographic distribution by Indian state

Data Parameters

  • • Sample: ~58,000 Claude.ai conversations from India
  • • Period: November 2025
  • • Published: February 16, 2026
  • • Context: Released at India AI Impact Summit

Caveats: Claude's user base skews technical and English-speaking. India-specific brief uses different methodology than global report (no randomised sample). Self-selected users, not representative of general population. O*NET mapping designed for US occupations: may not perfectly capture Indian job categories.

Source 2: OpenAI Signals, India

What It Measures

  • • ChatGPT platform usage data (WAU, message volume)
  • • Age demographics of Indian users
  • • Feature-specific engagement (Codex, data analysis, voice)
  • • Free vs paid tier patterns

Data Parameters

  • • Scale: 100M+ weekly active users in India
  • • Rank: #2 global market
  • • Published: February 20, 2026
  • • Context: Alongside Tata 100MW data centre, Mumbai/Bengaluru offices

Caveats: Data selectively presented alongside major commercial announcements (Tata deal, new offices, PM Modi meeting). No per-capita data provided. "Signals" is a PR-infused data release, not a peer-reviewed study. Free tier dominates India's 100M WAU: paid conversion unknown.

Source 3: Google/Ipsos "Our Life With AI" Survey

What It Measures

  • • Self-reported AI usage, knowledge, and attitudes
  • • Trust in AI accuracy, workplace impact, learning intent
  • • Sentiment (promising vs concerning scales)
  • • Cross-country comparison (21 countries)

Data Parameters

  • • Sample: ~21,000 adults (18+), ~1,000 per country
  • • Countries: 21 (including India)
  • • Period: Sep 22 to Oct 10, 2025
  • • Published: January 15, 2026

Caveats: Online sampling methodology inherently skews towards urban, educated, digitally connected respondents, especially significant in India where 500M+ lack regular internet access. ~1,000 respondents cannot represent 1.4 billion people. Self-reported usage often over-estimates actual behaviour. Google-sponsored survey may have framing bias towards positive AI narratives.

Source 4: Microsoft AI Diffusion Report (H2 2025)

What It Measures

  • • Cross-platform AI penetration normalised per capita
  • • Adjusted for OS/device market share, internet penetration, population
  • • Not limited to Microsoft products
  • • Country-level rankings across 100+ nations

Data Parameters

  • • Coverage: 100+ countries
  • • Method: Aggregated, anonymised telemetry
  • • Published: January 8, 2026
  • • India rank: #64 per-capita AI adoption

Caveats: Most methodologically rigorous for cross-country comparison, but telemetry still depends on Microsoft ecosystem footprint. Adjustments for OS market share and internet penetration are modelled, not directly observed. "AI adoption" definition may differ from other sources.

The Convergence Principle

When 3-4 independent sources, using different methodologies, measuring different things, with different biases, point in the same direction, the signal is strong. We found 9 such convergences across these 4 platforms, documented in each section of this report.
