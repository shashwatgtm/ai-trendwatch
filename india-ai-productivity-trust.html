🤝 Trust: Behavioural + Attitudinal Convergence

Behavioural Trust (Anthropic)

Autonomy Delegation

3.60 /5 (vs 3.38 global)

Indian users give Claude more decision-making freedom than the global average — they trust the AI enough to let it operate with less human oversight.

Attitudinal Trust (Google/Ipsos)

Trust AI Accuracy "A Great Deal"

45% vs 16% global

Nearly 3x the global rate. When asked about AI accuracy, Indians express significantly higher confidence — aligning with the informed optimism pattern from Report #1.

💡 The Strategic Takeaway

Indian users aren't just using AI more — they're delegating more authority to it. This dual trust signal (behavioural + attitudinal) creates a faster path to enterprise agentic AI adoption in India than in trust-cautious Global North markets. For AI vendors, India is a market where you can sell autonomous AI features that would face resistance in Europe or Japan.
