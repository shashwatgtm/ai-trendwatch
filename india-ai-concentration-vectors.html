80% of ChatGPT messages from under-30s. This generational concentration will compound as these users age into decision-making roles.

The Strategic Takeaway

The three concentration vectors create a clearly defined addressable market: young software professionals in 4 metro clusters. This is not India's 1.4B population: it's roughly 20% of the working-age population. For GTM, this concentration is both the current opportunity and the future expansion frontier.
