Cross-Study Convergence: 9 Findings

1. Scale is Real: Confirmed 3 Ways

Anthropic #2 by total Claude use (5.8%) · OpenAI #2 by ChatGPT WAU (100M+) · Ipsos #1 in self-reported AI usage (86%)

2. Per-Capita is Middling: Confirmed 2 Ways

Anthropic #101/116 for Claude per-capita · Microsoft #64/100+ for cross-platform AI diffusion

3. Work-First Orientation: Confirmed 3 Ways

Anthropic 51.3% work (vs 46%) · OpenAI 35% professional (vs 30%) · Ipsos 80% used AI for work (vs 70%)

4. Coding/Software Dominance: Confirmed 2 Ways

Anthropic 45.2% software tasks (#1 globally) · OpenAI Codex 3x global, data analysis 4x global

5. Trust: Behavioural + Attitudinal: Confirmed 2 Ways

Anthropic 3.60/5 autonomy delegation (vs 3.38) · Ipsos 45% trust accuracy "a great deal" (vs 16%)

6. Productivity Above Global: Confirmed 3 Ways

Anthropic 15x speedup (vs 12x) · OpenAI 4x data analysis intensity · Ipsos 79% saved time (vs 65%)

7. Geographic Concentration: Confirmed 2 Ways

Anthropic Maharashtra, TN, Karnataka, Delhi = 50%+ · OpenAI Telangana, Karnataka, TN = top coding states

8. Youth Dominance: OpenAI Exclusive

OpenAI 50% of ChatGPT messages from ages 18-24 · 80% from under-30

9. The Aspiration Gap: Ipsos Exclusive

Ipsos 64% very interested in learning AI · 44% believe they need to learn in next 5 years · 42% want more confidence

The Core Paradox

India isn't one AI market. It's two. The elite 20% that's #1 globally. And the 80% that's still at the starting line. Four platforms agree: the scale is real, the concentration is real, and the opportunity lies in bridging the gap.

"Anthropic shows what Indians do with AI. OpenAI shows who does it and at what scale. Google/Ipsos shows what they think and feel. Microsoft shows where India sits on a normalised global basis. Together, they're the most complete picture of any country's AI adoption ever assembled."

Shashwat Ghosh, Co-Founder and Fractional CMO, Helix GTM Consulting

Context: India AI Impact Summit (Feb 16-20, 2026)

All four reports were released around the first global AI summit hosted in the Global South (New Delhi). This timing is not coincidental.

Methodology: 4-Source Triangulation

Anthropic Economic Index

~58,000 Claude.ai conversations from India (Nov 2025). Tasks mapped to O*NET occupation codes. Measures actual usage patterns. Limitation: Claude's user base skews technical and English-speaking.

Published: Feb 16, 2026

OpenAI Signals

ChatGPT platform data from India's 100M+ WAU base. Released at India AI Impact Summit alongside commercial announcements. Data real but selectively presented: no per-capita data.

Published: Feb 20, 2026

Google/Ipsos Survey

Online survey, ~21,000 adults (18+), 21 countries, Sep 22 to Oct 10, 2025. ~1,000 Indian respondents. Online sampling skews urban and digitally connected, caveat for a country where 500M+ lack regular internet.

Published: Jan 15, 2026

Microsoft AI Diffusion

Aggregated, anonymised Microsoft telemetry adjusted for OS/device market share, internet penetration, and population. Most methodologically rigorous for cross-country comparison. Not limited to one product.

Published: Jan 8, 2026

Cross-study note: Comparing numbers across these sources requires caution: each measures different things. Directional convergence matters more than exact number matching.
